2026 M1 MIND Research Project · Sorbonne University / LIP6

Structured Outputs for LLMs: JSON Schema & Constrained Decoding

Experimental pipeline for improving the reliability of structured output generation with LLMs. Integrated constrained decoding with Llama-3.1, validated outputs with JSON Schema, and analyzed complex-schema limitations, timeouts and compliance errors.

2025 M1 MIND · Information Retrieval

Information Retrieval Engine: BM25 & Guided Traversal

Reimplemented a sparse search engine inspired by a SIGIR paper, including an inverted index, BM25 scoring, neural scores and Guided Traversal. Worked on posting lists, top-K retrieval, query-document scoring and efficiency-relevance trade-offs.

Information RetrievalBM25Guided TraversalTop-K Retrieval
2025 M1 MIND · DALAS

Road Accident Severity: Data Enrichment & Interpretable ML

Built a pipeline to study how weather conditions relate to road accident severity. Combined open accident data with historical weather data, automated collection with Selenium, processed data with PostgreSQL/PostGIS, then performed modeling and interpretability analysis.

Interpretable MLPostgreSQLPostGISSeleniumGeospatial Data
2026 M1 MIND · Kaggle Competition

Bioacoustic Classification: Multi-label Audio ML

Designed a multi-label audio classification pipeline for species identification from Pantanal soundscapes. Extracted classical audio descriptors, trained One-vs-Rest models across 234 classes, handled label imbalance and analyzed the focal-recording / test-data domain gap.
